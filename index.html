What We Do

An Integrated Vision
for Infrastructure

From land acquisition to landmark delivery — Siskin Projects participates across the full value chain of infrastructure and real estate development in India.

Civil Construction & Infrastructure

Delivering engineering excellence across residential, commercial, industrial, and public infrastructure sectors.

  • Roads & urban infrastructure
  • Industrial & commercial structures
  • Project management & EPC services
  • Public utility & engineering projects

Real Estate Development

Creating residential, commercial, and industrial properties designed for long-term value and urban demand.

  • Residential apartments & villas
  • Commercial complexes & office parks
  • Warehouses & industrial facilities
  • Mixed-use & retail developments

Land & Township Development

Strategic land acquisition, aggregation, and development for plotted schemes and integrated townships.

  • Plotted layouts & townships
  • Large-scale land aggregation
  • Community infrastructure & amenities
  • Joint development & monetization

Consultancy & Advisory

Technical expertise and strategic counsel across the infrastructure lifecycle — from feasibility to delivery.

  • Project planning & feasibility studies
  • Design coordination & oversight
  • Cost management & estimation
  • Infrastructure advisory services
Why Siskin

Built on Integrity,
Driven by Excellence

Every project we undertake reflects our commitment to quality, ethical practice, and results that stand the test of time. Here's what sets us apart.

01

Integrated Delivery Model

We operate across the full infrastructure value chain — construction, real estate, and land development — under a single, disciplined execution framework. No handoff risk. Total accountability.

02

Quality-First Approach

From material procurement to structural delivery, we set and maintain exacting quality standards. Our leadership has built landmark projects that have redefined benchmarks in their markets.

03

Ethical Business Practices

Transparency, compliance, and investor protection are not afterthoughts — they are foundational. We operate with the same standards we would demand of any partner.

04

Rated & Regulated Credibility

Siskin Projects carries an IVR BB / Stable credit rating from Infomerics Valuation and Rating Limited, and operates under full SEBI-compliant listed entity disclosure norms.
